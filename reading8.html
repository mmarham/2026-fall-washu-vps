Media Response 6

This is a media response for Do Artifacts have Ethics? by L.M. Sacasas.

This article feels incredibly relevant given the rise of AI in the present day. In some sense it seems like morality and ethics have been entirely removed from the development of AI when you hear CEO's and those higher up in these companies literally saying that they think the tech they are developing could lead to human extinction. When we think about how big the implications are for tech such as this, I think the magnitude of the consequences can feel so grand and still far off that its hard for us to act. That's why I appreciate this articles recentering on an individual experience and how using technology makes one feel and how it affects their interactions with those around them. When you ask these questions with AI in mind I think you often get negative answers, because we've seen cases of AI psychosis and people becoming entirely dependent on chatbots for social interaction. Even I feel myself becoming more dependent on AI when I need help with things and it sometimes makes me feel like I'm losing my own intelligence. Ultimately I think the questions this article poses are very important to consider and can make us reconsider the value of technology and the way in which we use them in our daily lives.
