Reading Response 4

This is a reading response for the documentary "Eno" on the life and thoughts of creative Brian Eno.

I hadn't known who Brian Eno was before this documentary so watching it was very eye opening. I think its very interesting to see how concepts he introduced into music still exist and have become more popular, such as sampling. Sampling now a days is very common especially in rap and hip hop and I think it relates to a statement he made about how bringing something into a new context is essentially creating something entirely new. Another part that resonated with me was his music that had spatial inspirations, such as the airport music. His interpretation on what an airport should sound like had much more of a deeper meaning and intent than what I had expected. The concept of generative music was also very new to me, and I thought his metaphor which compared it to evolution was especially provocative. I wonder how this kind of music works in the day to day, like are the music players we are accustomed to able to play things like that. I was also interested in how this relates to the idea of generative AI and parametric architecture, where you are also prescribing guidelines through which something is created. The way he viewed this scenario felt very unique in the sense that it wasn't in pursuit of optimization but instead endless variety.
