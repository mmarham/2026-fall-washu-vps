Reading Response 5

This is a reading response for this interview with artist Damon Zucconi.

This process of creation that comes from collecting reference materials online feels super familiar to me because many people I interact with in creative fields and even myself are always ambiently collecting things they find interesting or inspiring. Often on things like Pinterest, Instagram, or more simply through screenshots or photos. I think its really interesting and sensible that Damon uses these collections of references as context for the artwork he makes, because being able to view what an artist finds interesting would definitely help me appreciate and maybe understand their own art more. I think the conversation on ownership over reference material is also interesting, I don't know if I fully agree with the feeling of ownership over material you find on the internet. However I can understand the idea of ownership over the fact that you were able to find a specific piece of material or inspiration from the near limitless stuff online.
