Writing Exercise 1

Once a week me and my friends get together and cook dinner at one of our apartments. This has been incredibly important to me because in my initial years at WashU I had struggled to maintain contact with friends in other majors since architecture is so time consuming and isolating. We never really know what we're going to make when we get their and its always very off the cuff-whatever's at hand-kind of vibe. But its always super low stakes, relaxing, joyous, even when things are going wrong or we're burning stuff, and its a really amazing respite when life is stressful. We also make pretty good food most of the time, the meals we make are usually super indulgent and not very health oriented, and we all save good ingredients or cuts of meat for these dinners. I think this practice tells us that humans need others, and balance, in order to be fulfilled.
