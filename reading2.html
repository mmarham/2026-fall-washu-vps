Reading Response 2

This is a reading response for this article on the new Turing test.

I find myself in strong agreement with this piece, and its stance on how pieces of media should be treated whether created by AI or not. I think its also important to remember that large language models and machine learning algorithms are essentially just systems of pattern mimicry that as the author states, lacks individuality because of the nature in how they are trained. In most cases I also assume the AI slop that populates our social media feeds are designed to attract the most attention and cater to the largest number of viewers that they can and in doing that they continue to lose the human feel that organic creations can have. When the author was talking about the responses that people may get when confronting a creator about AI use and they get either denial or are told that it doesn't matter, I felt that this kind of dialogue and process within media today is very saddening.
