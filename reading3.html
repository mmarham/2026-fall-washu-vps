Reading Response 3

This is a reading response for this article on the possibilities of a website.

I thought this essay was very interesting especially in the metaphors it offered for websites one could create. One that stuck out to me in particular was the idea of a website as a puddle. I liked the quote "If the world doesn't end tomorrow, there will be another storm. And when there's a hole, a puddle will appear again." Personally I feel like I often think about projects such as a website with the lens of a perfectionist by believing that a website I make should be this singular product that is created to the peak of my abilities. I think its much more freeing to view a website as a much more transient and almost casual experience. By creating your own website you are in full control of the experience a user goes through, whereas if someone else makes it for you it can only be an attempted translation of your own thoughts and wishes.
