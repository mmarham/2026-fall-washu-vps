Media Response 6

This is a media response for this digital garden by Chia Amisola.

Playing through Chia Amisola's "There are No Gardens on the Internet", reminded me a lot of old point and click games where you would try to find items in the environments on screen in order to work towards solving a mystery or progressing through a story. In the case of Amisola's piece it seemed like you were playing through to both collect items and little quotes and poems regarding digital gardens. I played through for a little while and then opened the map and realized how sprawling this garden really was. The way the pages of the garden shift as you click through them and the paths loop back onto themselves made the experience feel very psychedelic and a tad overstimulating. I appreciated the intentionality in the design of things like the inventory and the record of all the text pieces that you had collected. My one gripe was that the experience was a bit confusing, maybe by design, I wasn't really sure if the items I was collecting were meant to be used and I was also a little lost on the overarching story of the piece. I also cannot comprehend what connection this piece has to Hyaundai but its very cool that they sponsor this kind of art.
