Writing Exercise 4

I appreciate reading physical books. I was a really big reader growing up and I have a lot of memories of just spending days immersing myself in books I would get from the library. Nowadays I think a combination of not being able to find time and it simply being more easy or convenient to just read off my phone or listen to an audiobook has made it so that I really haven't read a physical book in a long time. I think its a better way to consume written media and I'm just nostalgic for my childhood so I hope to reconnect with these habits at some point. I think one lesson I can pull from this is that even with things getting much faster, easier, and convenient for us in the present day, there's a lot of value in slowing down and doing things more analog.
