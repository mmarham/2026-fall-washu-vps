Writing Exercise 3

I cherish doing creative activities the slow or hard way. Activities like designing, writing, or making art, are things that I feel need to be done in an iterative and thoughtful mindset. When the process itself is enjoyable and necessary I think you gain as much fulfillment through creation as you do upon completion. In many of these kinds of tasks you are also trying to impart some aspect of your personality or thoughts in a creative medium and doing so in a satisfactory way usually takes a lot of time. By rushing through it you're only compromising the final product so cherishing the climb becomes the only way to produce something good and true to your intent. I think this shows us that humans naturally draw fulfillment from the creative process and its only when the value statement of art gets flipped that we choose to rely on technology that can expedite the process.
