Reading Response 1

This is a reading response for this conversation about restaurant websites from 2019.

I have never really thought about restaurant websites in depth to be honest and I don't remember ever encountering one special enough to pique my interest. I can see the appeal behind websites such as the one for Astro, where it has a very personal and nostalgic feeling, and the other examples given which feel a bit more professional and artistic. I think the whole conversation between this sort of handmade website versus something created through a different platform like Squarespace feels a lot like the AI vs. human debate. Where using a tool like AI may make things easier and more accessible to the general public, but also makes them feel more impersonal and similar. By making website creation more accessible they also take the struggle and learning out of the process of learning to code which could either be good or bad depending on how you view it. One of the ideas I really liked from this conversation was a restaurant website having a menu archive, I think that's super interesting because of the way it can show a restaurant changing over time and seasonality.
